About Us

MtFanChuan Store is an independent online shop owned and operated by ZHITING LIU, based in China, preparing to sell handicrafts, apparel and digital goods to buyers worldwide. The store is currently in preparation: the catalog on this page is a preview, and no orders are being accepted yet. We will announce the opening here once sales begin.

Contact Us

For questions about the store, please reach us through our PayPal page: paypal.me/MtFanChuan. We aim to reply within 2 business days once the store opens.

Privacy Policy

This website does not collect accounts, passwords or payment information. No orders are processed on this site at present, so no personal data is collected through checkout. If a contact message is sent through our linked PayPal page, only the information the sender provides there is used to reply. This policy will be updated when sales open.

Refund Policy

No orders are accepted and no payments are taken while the store is in preparation, so there is nothing to refund at this stage. Once sales open, the following will apply: buyers may request a refund within 7 days of delivery for damaged or wrongly shipped physical items; digital downloads, once delivered, are non-refundable unless the files are defective. Refunds, when granted, will be returned to the original payment method.

Shipping Policy

No shipments are made while the store is in preparation. Once sales open, physical items will be shipped from China by tracked mail; delivery times and shipping fees will be shown at checkout and vary by destination. Digital items will be delivered by download link to the buyer's email after payment. Buyers are responsible for any import duties or taxes in their own country.
